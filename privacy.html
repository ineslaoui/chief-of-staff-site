Privacy policy

Chief of staff · Last updated 2026-11-15

Chief of staff is a personal app with one user, its owner. This policy explains how it accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

What it accesses

Only the Google account its owner signs in with, and only with the permissions she grants on Google's consent screen: her Gmail (to read, search, draft and send email) and her Google Calendar (to read, add and change events).

How it uses that data

Only to do what its owner asks: summarize her email, save drafts for her review, send messages she has asked for, and keep her calendar. It doesn't use Google user data for advertising, and it doesn't sell it.

Where it's stored

The app runs on its owner's own computer. Google's sign-in tokens are stored there, readable only by her user account. Notes the app keeps for her stay on that computer.

Who it's shared with

To carry out a request, the app passes the relevant email or calendar content to the AI service its owner uses, Anthropic's Claude, under her own Claude account. It isn't shared with anyone else, and the app itself doesn't use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

Chief of staff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and delete its stored tokens from her computer.
