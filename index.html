Chief of staff

A personal assistant app that helps its owner keep up with her own email and calendar.

What it does

Chief of staff runs on its owner's own computer. With her permission, given through Google's sign-in, it uses the Gmail and Google Calendar APIs to:

  • read and search her email, so it can tell her what needs her attention;
  • save draft replies for her to review, and send email only when she asks for that message;
  • read her calendars, check when she's free, and add or change events she asks for.

Who it's for

This is a personal app with one user: its owner. It isn't offered to the public, and there's nothing to sign up for.

Your data

How the app handles Google user data is set out in the privacy policy. Use of the app is covered by the terms of service.
